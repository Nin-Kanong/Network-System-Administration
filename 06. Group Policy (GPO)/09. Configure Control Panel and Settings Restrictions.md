## Create a New GPO for HR

We will create a dedicated Group Policy Object.

In Group Policy Management:
1. Find HR under Staff.
2. ``Right-click`` on **HR**.
3. Select: ``Create a GPO in this domain, and Link it here...``

<img width="1917" height="877" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c48eded6-6600-404d-bfbe-a97bfceab9ee" />


A window called New GPO will appear, then enter:
````
GPO_HR_Block_Control_Panel_and_Settings
````

<img width="482" height="232" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/577ff2e0-c537-43d8-a61e-f985b5fb210f" />


Now we see our new GPO:

<img width="1917" height="677" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8264f89-eede-433d-a526-71bf25e9997e" />







### Edit the GPO
Now we will configure the restriction.

Right-click n ``GPO_HR_Block_Control_Panel_and_Settings``, Select ``Edit``

<img width="1917" height="705" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c08a81d-27af-4fd9-9281-628b6b559b38" />



#### Navigate to the correct policy

Follow this exact path:
````
User Configuration
    │
    └── Policies
        │
        └── Administrative Templates
            │
            └── Control Panel
````
Click **Control Panel**.


Now we see ``Prohibit access to Control Panel and PC settings``:

<img width="1917" height="877" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3524efed-fc79-4bb4-9c63-c99e8ca47688" />



1. Then `Double-Click` on `Prohibit access to Control Panel and PC settings`.
2. Select `Enabled`:
3. Then click `Apply`, and click `Ok`:

<img width="1102" height="792" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1c90a051-7d5c-41ec-96c1-482f6ddcaba0" />


Now we see it `Enabled`:

<img width="1615" height="866" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9640b831-98ad-4425-bc28-9113bf8f53df" />



> Windows prevents the Control Panel and PC Settings programs from starting for affected users.

## Apply Group Policy to HR Users
Creating and linking the GPO does not mean we currently logged-in user has immediately refreshed the new policy.

**Log in as an HR user**:
On our **Windows 11 client**:
1. **Sign out** of the current account.
2. Select **Other user**.
3. **Log in** using an HR domain account.
